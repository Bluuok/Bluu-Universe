# Selected Ultimate Space Kit assets

Quaternius — Ultimate Space Kit (March 2023), CC0 1.0.

Creator source: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatespacekit.html
Original author distribution: https://poly.pizza/bundle/Ultimate-Space-Kit-YWh743lqGX
Downloaded public GLB conversion pack: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/ultimate_space_kit-glb.zip
Conversion redistribution record: https://opengameart.org/content/ultimate-space-kit-by-quaternius
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Only seven models are included: two rocks, a plant, spiral tree, roof antenna, solar panel, and floating robot. Actual file bytes, triangles, node names, clips and SHA-256 checksums are in manifest.json. The pack animal Astronauts were examined but excluded from the project because their exposed animal heads and guns do not match the selected closed-visor protagonist.
